BLACK MOON SOVEREIGN — LATEST ASSET COLLECTION

Collected from generated assets in this conversation. One selected revision per motion/asset category, with a separate legacy 8-bit style folder.

Structure:
01_Master: phase 1 and phase 2 visual masters, with/without giant floating fists.
02_Phase1_Body_16f: intro, idle, walk, command, beam body, hurt, kneel.
03_Transformation: phase 1 to phase 2.
04_Phase2_Body_16f: berserk idle, walk, command, beam body, hurt, kneel, ultimate, death.
05_Gauntlets: phase 1 and phase 2 parts, fallen, merged ultimate.
06_Effects: fist effects and most recent combined VFX sheet with beams.
07_Retro_8bit: alternative pixelized assets, kept separately, not interchangeable with main style.

Notes:
* Generated sheets have not been checked frame-by-frame for exact anatomy, alignment, or color-key purity.
* Some sheets named BodyOnly may still contain unintended effects depending on the generated artwork; inspect before runtime use.
* Most character motion sheets are 4x4 (16 frames); some gauntlet and VFX sheets have different layouts.
* Background is chroma green, effects black. Remove backgrounds as needed before importing.
* Source filenames and SHA256 checksums are in MANIFEST.csv.
